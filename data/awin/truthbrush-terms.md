## Full Program Terms (read 16 Oct 2026, document last updated 31 May 2020)
Source: Awin programme page, Program Terms and Conditions download. The document itself is not kept in the repo; this is our summary.

**Commission**
- Excludes VAT and delivery. Credit card fees and gift-wrap are included.
- No products or categories are excluded.
- Commission is declined for: wholesale orders (Truthbrush reserve the right to refuse them), returned orders, cancelled or unpaid orders, minimum order not met, and orders not attributed to us.

**Paid search (Google Ads and similar)**
- No commission on paid search activity for their brand terms. Keyword "Truthbrush" makes paid search ineligible.
- Do not use their brand name in ad display URLs, titles or descriptions, and do not bid on brand variants or misspellings.
- Deduplicated against PPC brand activity and email advertising.
- Our site does not run paid search on Truthbrush. Keep it that way, or ask Truthbrush first.

**Branding**
- Use Truthbrush photography and logo only. Their logo needs their permission (brand cards use a text placeholder until then).

**Other**
- 7-day closure notice period: they can end the programme with 7 days' notice.
- Allowed partner types include content, newsletters, comparison services, cashback and direct linking.

**Impact on the site**
- Truthbrush product photos come from the feed (their own photography), which is permitted.
- Do not describe Truthbrush's commission or pricing as fixed; prices are as shown in the feed, dated when checked.
