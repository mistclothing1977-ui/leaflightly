# Competitor cross-reference, 16 Oct 2026

Source: "Comparative Publisher Architecture and Monetization Audit" (Eco Affiliate research folder, Oct 2026). This note uses the audit's observations. I have not revisited these sites today. Where the audit did not establish something, it is marked "not established".

Sixteen publishers are covered: the ten eco and lifestyle comparables and the six others in the audit.

## Eco and lifestyle comparables (10)

| Site | What they do well (per audit) | Where we differ | What to take | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leif List | Cross-sector gallery plus values navigation | No values-based navigation | Values filters (plastic-free, vegan, B Corp) on the all-products page | High |
| Good On You | Brand ratings, discovery filters, alternatives | Brand pages show claims but no "alternatives" | "Similar brands" on brand pages; keep claims separate from our check | Medium |
| Sustainable Jungle | Brand directory across many categories | One brand live | Brand index page once five brands are live | Medium |
| Ethical Consumer | Parent-company context and category-specific criteria | No ownership information | Parent-company line on brand pages when known | Low |
| The Good Trade | Values-led storytelling and evidence badges; clear "Advertise" link | Evidence badges are not explained | Explain each badge on hover or tap; link the method | High |
| The Spruce | Compare table and tested shopping guides | No comparison view from cards | Link the compare page from product cards | High |
| Good Housekeeping | Testing provenance, specifications, wishlist | No specifications or test source on product pages | Specifications block; say where information came from | High |
| Byrdie | Beauty expertise and editorial presentation | Five curated beauty products | Beauty guide once beauty brands are approved | Medium |
| RTINGS | Evidence depth, test data, change log | No change log for prices or claims | "Checked [date]" on prices and claims (dates already stored) | High |
| OutdoorGearLab | Trade-offs and transparent testing method | No trade-off wording on cards | One "best for / trade-off" line per product | Medium |

## Other publishers in the audit (6)

| Site | What they do well (per audit) | Where we differ | What to take | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Slickdeals | Deal lifecycle, feedback and repeat visits | No deal or price-change history | Show when a price last changed (we store the check date) | Medium |
| NerdWallet | Comparable attributes and clear destination labels | Buy links do not say where they go | Label every buy link with the destination: "Buy on [brand] (leaves Leaf Lightly)" | High |
| TechRadar | Regional multi-merchant offer modules | One merchant per product | Show alternative retailers where a product has more than one feed | Low |
| Tom's Guide | Quick picks, use-case awards and offer choice | No "best for" picks on the homepage | A "quick picks" row by use case (for example "gifts under £20") | Medium |
| MoneySavingExpert | Editorial and commercial separation; asterisked paid links | Paid links not marked as paid | Mark commercial links with a consistent symbol and a footnote | High |
| RetailMeNot | Expiry management and retention concepts | No expiry or retention features | Not a priority; revisit when we have offers with end dates | Low |

## Patterns across the sixteen

1. **Dated evidence.** RTINGS and Good Housekeeping show where information came from and when. We store dates but do not show them.
2. **Clear buy labels.** NerdWallet and MoneySavingExpert say where a link goes and whether it is paid. Our buy button does not say it leaves the site.
3. **Comparison as a step.** The Spruce and RTINGS let readers compare. Our compare page exists but is not linked from cards.
4. **Values and sector navigation.** Leif List combines both. Our site is sector-only.

## Recommended order

1. Show the checked date on prices and claims (RTINGS, Good Housekeeping, Slickdeals).
2. Buy-link labels: destination and paid status (NerdWallet, MoneySavingExpert).
3. Explain the evidence badges and link the method (The Good Trade).
4. Link the compare page from product cards (The Spruce).
5. Values filters on the all-products page (Leif List).
6. "Quick picks" by use case (Tom's Guide).

Items 1, 2 and 3 can ship without new data. Items 4 and 5 are quick. Item 6 needs more products first.
