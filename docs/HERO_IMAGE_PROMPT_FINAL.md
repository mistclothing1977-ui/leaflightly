## **How to Use These Images with Midjourney**

### **Step 1: Gather Images**
1. Download 2-4 product images from the stock sites above
2. Save them to your computer
3. Choose your favorites (best lighting, clearest products)

### **Step 2: Upload to Midjourney**
1. Go to Discord (if using Midjourney there) or midjourney.com
2. Type `/imagine` (or click imagine button)
3. **Drag and drop your images** into the message box
4. Paste the prompt below the images

### **Step 3: Complete Prompt with Image References**
```
[Paste uploaded images here]

Use these product reference images as style and quality guides. 
Create a professional minimalist workspace scene incorporating similar products naturally.
Overhead view, golden hour lighting, warm wood desk, cream linen mat, sustainable aesthetic.
Professional lifestyle photography quality. Kinfolk Magazine style.

[Then paste the full prompt from above]

--ar 16:9 --quality 2 --s 750 --iw 0.6
```

Note: `--iw 0.6` tells Midjourney to weight the reference images at 60% importance (adjust 0.3-1.0 as needed)

### **Step 4: Generate & Refine**
- Midjourney generates 4 variations
- Pick your favorite
- Use U1-U4 to upscale
- Use V1-V4 to make variations
- Refine prompt if needed
