# Stage 3: Hero Image Generation Prompts

## Concept: "Workspace Essentials" (Idea B)

Professional lifestyle photography for sustainable product showcase. Use these prompts with **Midjourney**, **DALL-E**, or **Adobe Firefly**.

---

## **Prompt Option 1: Midjourney (Recommended)**

### High-Quality Lifestyle Generation

```
A serene, minimalist home workspace captured from directly overhead during golden hour morning light. 

SCENE COMPOSITION:
- Natural light wooden desk (light oak or ash wood finish, visible grain)
- Soft, warm sunlight streaming in from upper left
- Blurred window with soft daylight in background
- Minimalist, very organized aesthetic
- Clean, uncluttered composition
- Professional lifestyle photography quality

FEATURED PRODUCTS (positioned naturally):
- Premium stainless steel insulated water bottle (like Klean Kanteen, mirror-polished finish, positioned on left side, catching light)
- Organic cotton fabric or linen textile (natural beige/cream, draped loosely, shows fine weave texture)
- Small potted plant with vibrant green foliage (ceramic pot, minimal and modern)
- Handmade ceramic cup with warm coffee inside (cream/natural pottery color, steam barely visible)

ADDITIONAL DETAILS:
- Wooden desk accessories (pen holder, small wooden tray)
- Open notebook with subtle texture
- Soft shadows creating depth
- No people visible
- Minimal clutter - every object intentional

LIGHTING & MOOD:
- Golden hour directional light (warm, inviting)
- Soft shadows, no harsh contrast
- Warm color temperature (2800-3200K feel)
- Professional product photography aesthetic
- Inspirational but grounded, not overly styled

COLOR PALETTE (critical for brand):
- Wood tones: warm caramel and light oak (#C9B8A0 adjacent)
- Accents: forest green foliage (#1a472a hints)
- Neutrals: cream, soft white, natural linen tones
- Metals: brushed stainless steel highlights
- Zero artificial colors, all natural materials

STYLE & TONE:
- Professional lifestyle photography
- Modern minimalism
- Sustainable, eco-conscious aesthetic
- Premium, accessible, aspirational
- Honest and unmanufactured feeling
- NOT overly staged or artificial
- Photography by: Kinfolk Magazine or Kinfolk-inspired

TECHNICAL:
- 16:9 aspect ratio
- Sharp focus throughout
- Depth of field: slight blur on background only
- Color grading: warm, slightly desaturated, natural
- No visible watermarks or text

--ar 16:9 --quality 2 --s 750
```

**Midjourney Command:**
```

(iw = image weight, adjust 0-2 to control how much the reference image influences the output)
```

## **Step-by-Step: Using Midjourney**

1. **Go to**: midjourney.com or Discord (if you have access)

2. **Prepare images** (optional, for realism):
   - Screenshot product photos from Leaf Lightly site
   - Or provide product mockup images
   - Save as high-quality JPG/PNG

3. **Create Midjourney message**:
   ```
   /imagine [paste one of the prompts above]
   ```

4. **If using product images**:
   - Upload the images first (drag into Discord)
   - Add the prompt
   - Midjourney will reference them

5. **Generate variations**:
   - Use U1, U2, U3, U4 to upscale favorite versions
   - Use V1, V2, V3, V4 to create variations
   - Refine the prompt based on results

6. **Download & save**:
   - Right-click final image → Save image
   - Optimize to <200KB using tinypng.com

## **Prompt Refinement Tips**

### **If Generated Image is Too Cluttered:**
Add to prompt: `"minimal, only 4 key objects, lots of negative space, very organized"`

### **If Lighting is Wrong:**
Add: `"golden hour lighting from upper left, warm color temperature 3000K, soft shadows"`

### **If Products Don't Look Right:**
Add: `"premium quality products, professional product photography, high-end brands, luxury aesthetic"`

### **If Scene Looks Too Staged:**
Add: `"natural arrangement, lived-in but organized, unmanufactured aesthetic, honest composition"`

### **For Maximum Brand Consistency:**
Add: `"Kinfolk magazine aesthetic, sustainable lifestyle, eco-conscious, modern minimalism, warm and inviting"`

## **Expected Results**

✅ **Good Hero Image Will:**
- Show 3-4 recognizable products
- Have warm, professional lighting
- Display natural wood and materials
- Suggest sustainability without preaching
- Have excellent composition (overhead/flat lay)
- Feel premium and aspirational
- Work well with text overlay

❌ **Red Flags (Regenerate if You See):**
- Products look fake or plastic
- Lighting is harsh or artificial
- Too much clutter or chaos
- Clichéd "green" sustainability imagery
- Poor composition or unbalanced
- Looks overly staged or corporate
- Text overlay would be hard to read

## **Post-Generation Optimization**

### **Using TinyPNG (Free Compression)**
```
1. Go to tinypng.com
2. Upload generated image
3. Download optimized version
4. Verify file size <200KB
5. Verify quality still excellent
```

### **Using Photoshop (Add Text Overlay Testing)**
```
1. Open image
2. Add semi-transparent dark overlay (20-30% opacity)
3. Add sample text: "Built to last a lifetime"
4. Verify text readability and hierarchy
5. Save as optimized JPG
```
