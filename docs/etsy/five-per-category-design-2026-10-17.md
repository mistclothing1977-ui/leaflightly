# Etsy launch range: five products per category (design, 17 Oct 2026)

Decision (owner, 16 Oct 2026): launch with five products in each of the three collections, then test and review.

Status: design only. Nothing built or listed. Prices are test hypotheses, not market averages. Competitor references come from the earlier research pass and were not re-verified.

Shared rules for every product:
- Each product has one buyer, one job and one completed example.
- Printable interiors stay white; colour is used for headings and panels only (token list in the launch plan).
- Every product ships with a quick-start file, licence, support route and version number.
- No health, environmental or savings claims unless a method and evidence are supplied with the product.
- Five-person user test before listing. A product that fails the test is fixed or dropped.

## Collection 1: Low-Waste Home (core, buyer: households)

### 1. Pantry-First Meal Planner
- **Buyer:** a household that buys food it then wastes.
- **Job:** plan the week from what is already in the fridge, freezer and pantry, then write a top-up list.
- **Files:** A4, US Letter and low-ink PDF. 12 pages: stock sheets (fridge, freezer, pantry), use-first ingredient queue, seven-day plan with leftover destination, top-up list, one completed example.
- **Price test:** £6 to £10.
- **Differentiator:** starts from stock, not from recipes. Each leftover has a named next meal.
- **Claim guardrail:** no food-storage periods written in. Link to authoritative storage guidance instead.
- **User test:** build a week's plan from a supplied fictional stock list without help.

### 2. Room-by-Room Home Audit
- **Buyer:** a household that replaces things it already owns.
- **Job:** find three practical actions across kitchen, bathroom and laundry.
- **Files:** A4, US Letter and low-ink PDF. 12 pages: inventories, observation log, three-action decision page, four-week follow-up, one example.
- **Price test:** £6 to £9.
- **Differentiator:** ends in exactly three actions, one of which needs no purchase.
- **Claim guardrail:** no "chemical-free" scoring. Record product use, packaging and label information only.
- **User test:** choose three actions from a sample household without buying anything.

### 3. Buy, Repair, Borrow or Wait Planner
- **Buyer:** someone planning a purchase they are unsure about.
- **Job:** decide between keep, repair, borrow, buy used, buy new, or wait.
- **Files:** A4, US Letter and low-ink PDF. One-page decision tree, comparison worksheet (total cost and expected use), 7-day and 30-day wishlist review, two worked examples.
- **Price test:** £4 to £6.
- **Differentiator:** "Unknown" is a valid answer. No invented environmental score.
- **Claim guardrail:** a weighted preference is not a sustainability rating. Say so on the page.
- **User test:** complete both worked examples and one own purchase decision.

### 4. 30-Day Low-Waste Challenge
- **Buyer:** someone who wants a structured start.
- **Job:** one small action a day for 30 days, sequenced as observe, use up, maintain or repair, then decide.
- **Files:** A4, US Letter and low-ink PDF. 10 pages: daily action cards (one action, time estimate, cost category, checkbox, accessible alternative), weekly reflection, reusable monthly checklist.
- **Price test:** £5 to £8.
- **Differentiator:** "no purchase required" route on every week. "Adapted" and "not relevant" count the same as "done".
- **Claim guardrail:** no "30 days to zero waste" promise.
- **User test:** complete days 1 to 7 with a real household scenario.

### 5. Household Budget & Refill Tracker (Google Sheets, Excel edition later)
- **Buyer:** a household tracking spending on refills and utilities.
- **Job:** compare pack prices by unit price and track repair decisions.
- **Files:** Google Sheets view-only master with a copy link; PDF quick-start; worked example tab. Excel edition only after the Sheets version has passed testing.
- **Price test:** £12 to £18.
- **Differentiator:** refill unit-price and repair-cost tabs, not a general budget.
- **Claim guardrail:** utility savings shown as recorded spending, not forecasts. Estimated savings labelled as assumptions.
- **Build rule:** formulas only, no scripts in version one.
- **User test:** enter five transactions and explain the dashboard.

## Collection 2: Eco Creator Studio (separate buyer: creators and small businesses)

Build after the Low-Waste Home products have their first sales and feedback. Keep these on a separate listing set so the household buyer does not see creator products first.

### 6. Eco Social Post Kit (Canva, 18 templates)
- **Buyer:** a small sustainable business posting consistently.
- **Job:** produce evidence-led posts quickly.
- **Files:** Canva template link, PDF instructions. Instagram portrait 1080 × 1350. Six-slide carousel, practical tip, myth and evidence, product feature, FAQ, launch, offer and impact slides.
- **Price test:** £15 to £25.
- **Differentiator:** prompts such as "insert a verified result and source" rather than ready-made claims. Source and date fields on the impact slide.
- **Claim guardrail:** no prewritten environmental claims. Check Canva's current template-sharing rules before publishing.
- **User test:** a buyer on a free Canva account replaces text and images and exports two posts.

### 7. Eco Media Kit (two-page and one-page)
- **Buyer:** a creator pitching sustainable brands.
- **Job:** show audience fit, real work and metrics with definitions.
- **Files:** Canva template link; A4 and US Letter; one-page condensed version; instruction sheet.
- **Price test:** £9 to £15.
- **Differentiator:** every metric shows its reporting period, platform, source and denominator.
- **Claim guardrail:** sample metrics labelled "Example, replace before sending".
- **User test:** a creator completes the one-page version with their own figures.

### 8. Sustainable Product Guide Layout (24-page ebook template)
- **Buyer:** a small brand writing product care, repair or sourcing guides.
- **Job:** present product information with evidence, limits and references.
- **Files:** Canva template link; A4 and US Letter. Sections: care, repair, materials, sourcing, evidence callout, limitations, references.
- **Price test:** £18 to £25.
- **Differentiator:** structure for evidence and limitations, not just layout.
- **Claim guardrail:** the template has an evidence callout that must carry a source and date.
- **User test:** a buyer writes a one-page care guide using the template.

### 9. Partnership Pitch Pack (one-page brief and case-study layout)
- **Buyer:** a creator or small brand proposing a collaboration.
- **Job:** write a short, credible brief and one case study.
- **Files:** Canva template link; A4 and US Letter; enquiry email template; instruction sheet.
- **Price test:** £8 to £12.
- **Differentiator:** deliverables, timelines and permissions are on the first page.
- **Claim guardrail:** no fabricated results; fields require the buyer's own evidence.
- **User test:** complete a brief for a fictional collaboration.

### 10. Pinterest Pin and Carousel Kit (12 pins, 6 video covers)
- **Buyer:** a creator or small business using Pinterest for traffic.
- **Job:** produce pins in Pinterest proportions quickly.
- **Files:** Canva template link. Pinterest 1000 × 1500; vertical covers 1080 × 1920. Guide, tutorial, product and list variants.
- **Price test:** £8 to £12.
- **Differentiator:** pins written for search phrases such as "plastic free planner" and "zero waste checklist".
- **Claim guardrail:** verify crops and overlays in Pinterest before release.
- **User test:** a buyer produces three pins and one cover.

## Collection 3: Nature & Learning (buyer: home decorators, families, educators, crafters)

Standalone products, discoverable by visual search. Each needs a clear viewing context.

### 11. Botanical Wall Art Trio
- **Buyer:** someone decorating with coordinated neutral art.
- **Job:** three artworks in common frame sizes.
- **Files:** JPEG at 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 11:14 and A2, at 300 ppi. Size guide, printing instructions, personal-use licence.
- **Price test:** £7 to £12.
- **Differentiator:** original illustrations, composed separately for each ratio (not stretched).
- **Claim guardrail:** no claim that a digital download is inherently environmentally beneficial.
- **User test:** print one size and check crops and colour.

### 12. Composting Guide Poster (A3 and A4)
- **Buyer:** a household or classroom starting composting.
- **Job:** explain one named composting method: suitable inputs, unsuitable inputs, preparation, troubleshooting.
- **Files:** A3 and A4 PDF; source sheet with named guidance.
- **Price test:** £4 to £6.
- **Differentiator:** one named method, stated scope, troubleshooting section.
- **Claim guardrail:** local or provider exceptions noted; no universal composting claim.
- **User test:** a teacher or parent uses it to set up one bin.

### 13. Seasonal Produce Guide (UK, A3 and A4)
- **Buyer:** a household buying seasonal food.
- **Job:** see what is typically in season by month.
- **Files:** A3 and A4 PDF; month grid; source sheet.
- **Price test:** £4 to £6.
- **Differentiator:** UK-specific, with a legend separating fresh from stored produce, and a variability note.
- **Claim guardrail:** sources and date of check on the footer. Do not claim seasonality is the same across the UK.
- **User test:** a buyer identifies three in-season items for the current month.

### 14. Recycling Symbols Guide (A4)
- **Buyer:** a household checking packaging.
- **Job:** identify symbols and check local collection.
- **Files:** A4 PDF; local-check checklist; source sheet.
- **Price test:** £3 to £5.
- **Differentiator:** says what a symbol does not establish, and how to check the local council.
- **Claim guardrail:** no universal "recyclable" statement.
- **User test:** a buyer identifies five packaging symbols and the local check step.

### 15. Family Nature Journal Printable (Letter and A4)
- **Buyer:** families and educators running nature walks or seasonal activities.
- **Job:** record observations, sketches and questions over a season.
- **Files:** A4 and US Letter PDF; 12 pages: seasonal prompts, species and weather log, sketch pages, reflection, one example entry.
- **Price test:** £4 to £7.
- **Differentiator:** prompts that work for mixed ages, with a teacher note.
- **Claim guardrail:** no species identification claims; prompt to check identification with a field guide.
- **User test:** a family completes one outing page.

## Launch sequence

1. Build the four Low-Waste Home products that cost least to test first: Meal Planner, Home Audit, Decision Planner, Challenge. Then the Sheets tracker.
2. Build the Nature & Learning set in parallel where the work is shared (same design system, same palette).
3. Build the Creator Studio set after the first Low-Waste sales, unless creator demand appears earlier.

Gates: each product passes its user test before listing. Review every product at 30 days using visits, sales, support questions and refunds.
