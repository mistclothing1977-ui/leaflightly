## 3. Digital product (first listing)

**Working title:** Plastic-Free Home Planner (printable PDF)

**What it is:** a printable monthly planner for reducing single-use plastic at home: a monthly checklist, a shopping list by category (kitchen, bathroom, cleaning, food storage), a "swap tracker" and a short section on reading eco labels in general terms.

**Why it fits:** it matches our curation themes (kitchen, home, wellness) and gives a buyer something useful on its own. It does not sell any product listed on leaflightly.com.

**Format:** A4 and US Letter PDF, 12 pages, print-friendly, plus an undated version. Two colourways (forest green and off-white).

**Price (starting point):** £4.50 to £5.50, to be tested. Check the listing fees and Etsy's transaction fee before fixing the price.

**Listing copy (draft):**
- Title: "Plastic-Free Home Planner | Printable Monthly Eco Checklist | A4 & Letter PDF"
- Description: what's inside, page count, how to print, the licence line (personal use only), and a note that the planner was made by Leaf Lightly.
- Tags (up to 13): plastic free planner, printable eco planner, zero waste checklist, sustainable living printable, eco home planner, reusable swap tracker, plastic free shopping list, zero waste planner, sustainable home printable, eco habits planner, green living printable, plastic free kitchen, eco printable pdf.

**Files to make (David or I can draft):** the planner PDF, a short "How to print" page, and a licence page. Keep the licence and the planner free of any Leaf Lightly website address, to stay clear of the link rules. The About page is the only place the website appears.

## 4. Compliance checks before launch

- Nothing in the listing, the files or the message to buyers points buyers to the website for a purchase, offers a discount for buying off Etsy, or uses a QR code (Off-Platform Transactions Policy, updated 5 Oct 2026).
- The product is made by us (Etsy requires digital items to be made or designed by the seller).
- The About page website line is plain text only.
- Confirm with Etsy seller support, in writing, that a website mention on the About page is allowed. Ask this before launch.

## 5. Measuring traffic

Etsy doesn't report referrals to our site unless a link is clickable. Two options:
- **Count shop visits and sales** in Etsy's own stats.
- **Ask Etsy** whether About-page text can carry a tracked link. Until then, the site can't measure Etsy-driven visits directly. Do not add untracked links for measurement.
