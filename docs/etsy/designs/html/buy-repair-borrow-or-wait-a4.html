Before you decide

Ask first: do I already own something that does this job?

  1. Describe the need.
  2. Choose your options.
  3. Note the total cost and expected use.
  4. Wait, then decide.

"Unknown" is a valid answer. If you do not know something, write it down rather than guessing.

Decision tree

Which route?

  • I already own something that works: use it
  • It just needs fixing: repair
  • I need it only once: borrow or hire
  • A used version is fine: buy used
  • A new one is needed: buy new, after the wait

Examples

Worked examples

Completed example

Small recurring purchase: a kitchen cleaner

Total cost over a year: £18 across 12 bottles. Use: weekly. Cost per use: about 35p. Refill option: available. Decision: buy a concentrate and refill.

Completed example

Durable item: a cast iron pan

Cost: £40. Expected use: several times a week for many years. Cost per use: low over time. Decision: buy, after checking that the pan you already own does not do the job.
