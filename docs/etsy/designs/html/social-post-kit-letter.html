How to use this kit

  1. Pick a post type from the list.
  2. Write the claim, the evidence and the source.
  3. Build the post in Canva from the matching template.
  4. Check the source and date before publishing.

Templates give you a structure. They do not supply facts. Every claim needs its own evidence.
