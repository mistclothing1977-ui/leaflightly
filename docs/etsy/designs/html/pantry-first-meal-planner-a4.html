How this planner works

  1. Fill in your fridge, freezer and pantry (5 minutes).
  2. Build meals from what you have first.
  3. Plan the week and note leftover destinations.
  4. Write a top-up list for only the gaps.
  5. Review the week and adjust next time.

Use what you have first. Buying less is the goal; a full plan is not required.
