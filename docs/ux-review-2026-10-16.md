# UX and conversion review, 16 Oct 2026

Scope: homepage, all-products page, Truthbrush brand page (desktop, 0.6 scale screenshots), product page text. Not yet reviewed: mobile, checkout-equivalent flows (Amazon/Awin links), signed-in pages, search results, compare, wishlist.

Assumptions to confirm: "earlier 10 brands" is not defined in the workspace. The cross-reference section below is a framework until you say which ten.

## Pass 1: the designer

1. **The hero headline sits on a busy video and is hard to read.** "DISCOVER PRODUCTS YOU CAN TRUST" is green on a dark-blue, moving background. The sub-line is small and white. Fix: add a scrim or solid panel behind the text, or move the copy onto a plain background. Add one clear primary button next to it.
2. **The first screen does not sell.** There is no product, no price, no proof. A first-time visitor sees a video, a slogan and a "Start exploring" button. Fix: show the six featured products (or a brand strip with logos) above the fold.
3. **Two search boxes on one screen.** The header has a search box and the all-products page repeats the same control. Pick one prominent search, or make the page-level one a filter.
4. **The affiliate disclosure sits at the top of the all-products page, above the products.** It is accurate but it reads as a warning before the product. Move it into the footer of the grid, or to a one-line note under the filters.
5. **The Truthbrush "The brand states" box is visually heavy.** It has a grey box, a border, a bold label and a disclaimer. Three styles compete. Fix: one quiet line for each claim, with the "not verified" note once.
6. **The "Our check" line uses the same box as the claims.** Verified and unverified information look alike. Fix: give verified findings a badge (for example a tick and the register name), and keep claims as plain text.
7. **The chat bubble covers content on every page.** It sits over the Truthbrush products heading and the lower product cards. Fix: collapse it to a small icon, or hide it on scroll.
8. **The developer "N" badge is visible bottom-left.** This must not appear on production. Check that it is dev-only.
9. **Heading hierarchy is inconsistent.** "Truthbrush products" is a large green heading, while "Our check" and "Options" are small. Fix: one scale (H2 for sections, H3 for sub-blocks).
10. **Product cards have no prices on the homepage.** Price is the first thing a shopper compares. Fix: show price and the tier badge on the card.
11. **Tier labels are unexplained on the card.** "Eco-Community Validated" and "Certified Eco" appear with no tooltip. Fix: a short hover or tap explanation, and link it to the tier page.
12. **Placeholder images say "Image coming soon".** Six out of six homepage products had this until today. Feed-sourced images now exist for Truthbrush; the rest still need photos. Fix: replace placeholders or use a plain brand-coloured tile with the product name, which looks intentional.
13. **The brand page has no call to action.** The only action is "Buy" on each product, which is buried below the fold. Fix: put the best-selling product and the shop button above the description.
14. **Brand name is repeated in the card and the heading on the brand page.** Small, but it adds noise. Fix: drop the repeat in the card.

## Pass 2: the first-time visitor

Walkthrough: home → "Start exploring" → all products → filter → brand card → brand page → product → buy.

- **Home:** I don't know what this site is for until I scroll. I see a slogan and a video. I'd leave if I didn't scroll.
- **Start exploring:** I expected to see products straight away. The next screen is a product list with filters, which is fine, but I don't know which products are good.
- **All products:** the affiliate note at the top made me pause ("are these ads?"). The filter panel is long and I don't know where to start. Sort and category are hard to find.
- **Brand card:** the card looks like a product card, not a brand. I expected a logo. I see a text name and "View brand". Unclear what I get.
- **Brand page:** "The brand states" is useful but confusing. I'd ask "so is it certified or not?". The "Our check" line answers part of that, but not clearly.
- **Product options:** the list of colours and bristle types is plain text with prices. I can't tell which option is best or whether they're all the same product. The first option is not highlighted.
- **Buy:** the button says "Buy on Truthbrush". I expected to buy on this site. It is not obvious I'm leaving the site.
- **Exit points:** the main points where I would leave are the home screen (no product on it), the all-products filter panel, and the brand page (no clear next step).

## Suggested steps (ordered by impact)

1. Put products and prices above the fold on the homepage, with a clear primary button.
2. Make the buy step explicit: "Buy on [brand] (leaves Leaf Lightly)" and place it above the fold on product pages.
3. Fix the hero text contrast and replace the video overlay with a scrim or plain background.
4. Replace placeholders with brand-coloured tiles until photos are available.
5. Rewrite the brand claims block: one verified line (with register name and date) and one plain list of claims.
6. Show prices and tier badges on cards; explain tiers on hover or tap.
7. Move the affiliate note out of the top of the all-products page.
8. Add a "best for" sort or featured filter so the first-time visitor has a starting point.
9. Collapse the chat bubble and hide the dev badge on production.
10. Mobile pass: review the same flow at phone width before any of the above ships.
