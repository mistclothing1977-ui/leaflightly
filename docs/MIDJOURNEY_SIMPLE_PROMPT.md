## **STEP-BY-STEP TO GENERATE**

### **Step 1: Save Your Reference Images**
- Save both workspace images you provided
- Keep them as high-quality files

### **Step 2: Open Midjourney**
- Go to **midjourney.com** 
- OR use Discord if you have Midjourney there

### **Step 3: Upload Your Images**
1. Click `/imagine` button
2. **Drag and drop BOTH workspace reference images** into the message
3. They'll appear as thumbnails

### **Step 4: Paste the Prompt**
- Copy the prompt above
- Paste it into the message below your images

### **Step 5: Generate**
- Press Enter or click Generate
- Wait 3-5 minutes for 4 variations

### **Step 6: Select Your Favorite**
- View the 4 generated images
- Click **U1**, **U2**, **U3**, or **U4** to upscale the best one
- Or click **V1-V4** for variations

4. Save as: `hero-background.jpg`
5. Add to: `/public/assets/images/hero-background.jpg`
6. Deploy to Vercel
