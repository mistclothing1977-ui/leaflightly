## **STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS**

### **Step 1: Gather the 4 Images**
Download these from the URLs above:
1. Water bottle image
2. Fabric/textile image  
3. Plant/flower image
4. Coffee cup image

Save all 4 to your computer.

### **Step 2: Open Midjourney**
- Go to midjourney.com OR Discord (if you have Midjourney there)
- Click `/imagine` button

### **Step 3: Upload Images**
- Drag and drop all 4 images into the message box
- They'll show as thumbnails in the message

### **Step 4: Paste the Prompt**
- Copy the prompt above (starting with `[UPLOAD THE 4...]`)
- Paste it below the images in the message

### **Step 5: Generate**
- Press Enter or click Generate
- Wait 2-5 minutes for Midjourney to create 4 variations

### **Step 6: Select & Upscale**
- View the 4 generated images
- Click `U1`, `U2`, `U3`, or `U4` to upscale your favorite
- Or click `V1-V4` to create variations of a promising result

### **Step 7: Refine (Optional)**
If the result isn't perfect:
- Click the upscaled image
- Click "Make Variations" or edit the prompt slightly
- Try again with adjustments
