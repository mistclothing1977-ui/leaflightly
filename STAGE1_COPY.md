## Tone Notes

### Voice & Style Decisions

**What We Sound Like:**
- Friendly and approachable (like texting a knowledgeable friend)
- Premium but not pretentious (Reformation energy, not luxury gatekeeping)
- Transparent and honest (acknowledge affiliate relationships, be real about what we do)

**What We Avoid:**
- Greenwashing language ("eco-friendly," "green," vague environmental claims)
- Corporate speak (no "we're thrilled to announce")
- False urgency ("LIMITED TIME," flashing banners)
- Jargon (no "sustainable fashion ecosystem")

**Key Principles:**
1. **Curation > Charity** — Position Leaf Lightly as a tastemaker, not a savior
2. **Active Voice** — "Browse our picks" not "our picks are available"
3. **Scannable** — Short sentences, max 12 words for trust signals
4. **Conversational** — Write like we're talking, not broadcasting
5. **Transparent** — Always name the affiliate relationship; trust is the real asset

**Copy Examples That Work:**
- ✅ "Free delivery on orders over £50" (specific, helpful, no greenwashing)
- ✅ "Returns within 30 days, no questions asked" (customer-centric)
- ✅ "We only recommend what we'd wear" (honest, relatable)

**Copy to Avoid:**
- ❌ "Shop sustainably" (vague, greenwashing)
- ❌ "Save the planet with every purchase" (false, pushy)
- ❌ "Limited eco-conscious selection" (implies scarcity, emphasizes limitation)
- ❌ "Join us in building a better future" (corporate, not personal)
